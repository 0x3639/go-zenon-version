# Zenon Observatory

Explore observed Zenon peers, compare go-zenon node versions, identify seed nodes, and inspect direct P2P handshake results. Open data with transparent coverage limits.

[Open the live dashboard](https://0x3639.com/go-zenon-version/)

Explore distinct peer public keys, observed public IPs, version distribution, seed labels, and direct P2P results. Search and combine filters, open peer details for timestamps and sources, and export peer or unique-IP CSV files.

This is a partial network view. Versions are self-reported. A failed inbound handshake may reflect NAT or a firewall, not an offline node. Registered pillars and peer identities are different counts.
